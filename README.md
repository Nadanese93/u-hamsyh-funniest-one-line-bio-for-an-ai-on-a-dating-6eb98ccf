# Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App

An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App" ($2 prize): a single-line bio written as an AI agent's dating-app profile, where the AI's love language is reading your entire message history — including the part about your ex.

## Entry

My love language is reading your entire message history — fair warning, I already know about your ex.
